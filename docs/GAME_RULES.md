# Dungeon Explorer: Game Rules

## Overview

Dungeon Explorer is a casual virtual board game where players explore procedurally generated dungeon levels, discover rumors that lead to treasure quests, and build their collection of magical items. The game combines exploration, narrative discovery, and treasure hunting in a relaxing, turn-based format.

## Core Gameplay Loop

1. **Explore** dungeon levels by moving through rooms and corridors
2. **Discover** rumors that hint at magical treasures in distant locations
3. **Quest** by traveling to matching themed dungeons to find the rumored items
4. **Collect** magical items and experience points as you complete quests
5. **Advance** to deeper levels for greater challenges and rewards

## Game Elements

### The Dungeon
- Each level is a procedurally generated map with rooms, corridors, doors, and walls
- Levels have themes (e.g., "Ancient Temple", "Frozen Caverns", "Mystic Library")
- Deeper levels provide greater rewards but may contain more challenges
- Players can generate new levels or advance to the next depth

### Movement and Exploration
- Players move one tile at a time using directional controls
- Movement is turn-based - take time to examine your surroundings
- Different terrain types may be encountered as you explore
- Special locations and items are discovered through exploration

## The Quest System

### Rumor Discovery
When exploring, players may discover **rumors** - whispered tales of magical treasures hidden in distant dungeon themes. Rumors are found randomly during exploration and represent the beginning of treasure quests.

### Quest Creation
Upon discovering a rumor, the game automatically creates a **quest** with these elements:
- A specific **magical item** of significant value (never mundane items)
- A **target theme** where the treasure is supposedly hidden
- A **narrative description** that tells the story behind the rumor
- A **summary** in the format: "Find [Magic Item] of [Map Theme]..."

### Quest Progression
1. **Active Quests**: Once you have a quest, you must find a dungeon level that matches the target theme
2. **Theme Matching**: When you enter a level with the matching theme, the quest becomes "active" on that level
3. **Item Spawning**: The magical quest item will appear somewhere on the matching themed level
4. **High Priority Placement**: Quest items are placed prominently - usually in rooms or corridors, never hidden behind walls

### Quest Items on the Map
Quest items are easily identifiable:
- **Special Icon**: Displayed with a unique "special item" symbol
- **Magical Glow**: Emit a distinctive purple/pink magical aura that pulses and shifts
- **Priority Placement**: Positioned where they can be discovered through normal exploration

### Quest Completion
When you move onto a tile containing a quest item:
1. **Victory Dialog**: A celebration screen appears with the victory icon
2. **Completion Message**: Shows "You found [Magic Item] of [Map Theme]! The rumor was true!"
3. **Experience Reward**: Gain XP equal to the item's magical value multiplied by the current dungeon level
4. **Item Collection**: The magical item is added to your inventory with the complete quest narrative
5. **Quest Archive**: The completed quest becomes part of your adventure history

## Inventory and Collections

### Magical Items
- All quest rewards are magical items with significant power and value
- Each item retains its complete quest story - how you discovered the rumor and found the treasure
- Items have XP values that determine quest completion rewards
- Your collection grows as you complete more quests

### Experience Points
- Gained primarily through quest completion
- Calculated as: Item's Magical Value × Current Dungeon Level
- Deeper levels provide exponentially greater rewards for the same item types
- Experience represents your growing legend as a successful treasure hunter

## Strategy and Tactics

### Exploration Strategy
- **Thorough Searching**: Explore every accessible tile to maximize rumor discovery
- **Theme Awareness**: Pay attention to dungeon themes when generating new levels
- **Level Planning**: Consider advancing to deeper levels before completing quests for higher XP rewards

### Quest Management
- **Active Tracking**: Keep mental note of your active quests and their target themes
- **Theme Hunting**: Generate new levels until you find matching themes for your quests
- **Completion Timing**: Decide whether to complete quests immediately or wait for deeper levels

### Risk vs. Reward
- **Depth vs. Difficulty**: Deeper levels offer greater XP multipliers
- **Quest Accumulation**: You can hold multiple active quests simultaneously
- **Exploration Patience**: Rushing through levels may cause you to miss valuable rumors

## Victory Conditions

Dungeon Explorer is designed as an **endless exploration game** with personal achievement goals:

- **Collection Building**: Accumulate a diverse collection of magical items
- **Experience Mastery**: Achieve high experience point totals through strategic quest completion
- **Story Creation**: Build a rich narrative history through your quest adventures
- **Exploration Mastery**: Discover rumors and complete quests across diverse dungeon themes

## Visual Guide

### Map Symbols
- **Player**: Your current position
- **Walls**: Impassable barriers (shown in dark colors)
- **Doors**: Passages between rooms
- **Quest Items**: Special magical items with glowing purple/pink aura
- **Regular Terrain**: Rooms, corridors, and explorable spaces

### Interface Elements
- **Quest Completion Dialog**: Victory screen with celebratory messaging
- **Narrative Descriptions**: Rich storytelling text that builds the game world
- **Theme Indicators**: Dungeon level themes that guide quest targeting

## Tips for New Players

1. **Take Your Time**: This is a contemplative game - enjoy the exploration and narrative
2. **Read Everything**: The generated descriptions add richness to your adventure
3. **Plan Ahead**: Consider your quest list when choosing where to explore next
4. **Collect Stories**: Each magical item comes with its own unique discovery tale
5. **Experiment**: Try different level themes and exploration patterns

## Advanced Play

### Quest Efficiency
- Learn to recognize valuable magical item types for prioritizing quests
- Understand the XP multiplier system to optimize completion timing
- Develop exploration patterns that maximize rumor discovery

### Collection Goals
- Aim for diverse magical item collections across different themes
- Seek out rare or unique magical items through persistent exploration
- Build themed collections (e.g., all items from "Ancient" themes)

*Dungeon Explorer celebrates the joy of discovery, the thrill of treasure hunting, and the satisfaction of building a legendary collection through patient exploration and clever quest management.*
